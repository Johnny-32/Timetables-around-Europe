# Timetables around Europe
This projects aims to show public transport timetables from all around Europe, that I think are interesting.

## List of included timetables:
- Berlin BVG
- Vienna Wiener Linien - bus/tram
- Copenhagen DOT - bus
- Odense FynBus - bus
- Oslo Ruter - bus
- Barcelona TMB
- Stockholm SL - bus and metro
- London TFL - online and on the stop
- Amsterdam GVB - buses **(this could be a template for my timetables)**, (how they have online and printable ones)
- Warsaw ZTM
- Paris RATP- frequent and infrequent bus lines
- Munich - bus

## Categories of timetables
- Only online and only next departures - Barcelona, Warsaw, Berlin kinda, London (kinda),
- Hours as one column - Berlin (on the bus stop), Vienna, Amsterdam, Warsaw, Munich (kinda)
- Each departure as a separate cell - Copenhagen, Odense, Oslo, Stockholm (bus), Paris line 101
- That include frequencies - Paris line 120, Berlin online, London on the stop, Stockholm metro
